Privacy Policy

CalcXpert — All-in-One Calculator

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Overview

CalcXpert ("the App") is a free utility application developed by Bikash Gupta. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information when you use it on your Android device.

Your privacy matters. CalcXpert does not collect, store, or share personal data. All calculations are performed locally on your device.

01. Information We Collect

CalcXpert does not collect any personal information. The App does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide personal details to use it.

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or contact information
  • Your location
  • Financial information entered into the calculators
  • Health-related inputs, such as BMI data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to us

All calculations are performed locally on your device and are not transmitted to any server by the App.

02. Internet Permission

CalcXpert does not require an internet connection and does not use the Android internet permission. The App is designed to work fully offline.

03. Third-Party Services

CalcXpert does not integrate third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or tracking services. The App does not share user data with third parties.

04. Children's Privacy

CalcXpert does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. Because the App does not collect personal information, it is designed with user privacy in mind.

Applicable children's privacy requirements may vary depending on the user's location and the App's intended audience.

05. Data Security

CalcXpert does not collect or transmit user data to its servers. Information you enter for calculations is processed locally on your device.

Please keep your device secure, as the security of your device and its locally stored information is outside the App's control.

06. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page along with a revised "Last updated" date.

We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about our privacy practices.
